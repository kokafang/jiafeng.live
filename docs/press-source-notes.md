## Rate Your Music: important distinction

The user confirmed that the remembered name was Rate Your Music (RYM).

Found: [a user-created 2020 list preserved on List Challenges](https://www.listchallenges.com/rateyourmusic-top-20-hyperpop-albums-2020). Emotional Dance Music is included as item 15. The compiler explains that the list used RYM's Bubblegum Bass category at the time, with exclusions, rather than a then-existing Hyperpop genre chart.

This is not an official RYM editorial feature, award, current ranking, or corroboration of a "top 10" claim. The original RYM page could not be verified. Removed from the website at the artist's request on 2026-09-08 because it is not an official source. The now-empty Archive filter was also removed. These research notes are retained for provenance only; do not restore the entry without approval.
