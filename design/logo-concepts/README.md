## Prompt Set

### A / initial

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: favicon / personal artist monogram for Jiafeng, candidate A, one standalone square image.
Primary request: design an original, very simple, instantly recognizable uppercase letter J with a subtle digital character.
Design: one continuous solid ivory J, tall straight stem on the right and a generous leftward bottom hook, short horizontal top cap. A carefully constructed stepped geometry: use just two clean rectangular steps at the bottom hook instead of a fully round curve. Optical balance, restrained and confident, like a contemporary electronic musician's independent label identity. The letter MUST unmistakably read J, not U, G, or a bracket.
Composition: single centered monogram occupying about 70% of the square canvas, ample 15% margins, plain uniform nearly-black deep teal background #0a151b, ivory mark #f7f3ea. Flat two-color silhouette, no tile border.
Constraints: only one J, no additional text, no wording, no grids or presentation mockups, no scene, no texture, no gradients, no glow, no 3D, no shadows, no tiny floating pixels. Strong weight and open negative space that remain clear at 16px. Crisp vector-like edges.

### A / clean refinement (selected)

Edit this J logo. Keep its overall uppercase J idea, positioning, short top cap, thick straight right stem and the stepped left bottom hook. Clean it into a perfect TWO COLOR graphic. Remove EVERY distressed/dirty/bleeding/speckled mark around and inside the glyph, remove all thin dark outlines. The background must be a single COMPLETELY OPAQUE solid blue-black #0a151b filling the entire square, not transparent. The J must be one flat ivory #f7f3ea solid silhouette, precisely cut crisp clean edges. Use only rectangular 90-degree stepped corners on the bottom hook, including the bottom-right corner (two coarse steps, not a rounded curve); preserve an instantly recognizable uppercase J. No texture, no small pixels, no gradients, no shadow, no glow, no fine details. This must work as a tiny favicon. Keep one single J, no other text.

### B / Tide

Use case: logo-brand. One standalone square favicon concept for the artist Jiafeng.
Make a simple custom uppercase letter "J", clearly legible at tiny size, in flat warm white on a COMPLETELY OPAQUE solid deep blue-black square background. This is candidate B: a fluid, soft, sculpted typographic J. The stem is upright on the right; the bottom turns into a generous leftward smooth curved hook and rises slightly at its left tip. A single continuous uniform weight silhouette, with elegantly rounded inner junction and a horizontal clean top edge. Contemporary and quiet rather than playful. Not a fish, not a U. Center the shape with 16% empty border all around.
Render as pristine flat vector artwork, two colors ONLY: #f7f3ea mark and #0a151b background, sharp antialiased edges. This is a graphic, NOT a photograph of a logo. Absolutely no outlines, no grain, no bleeding, no halo, no texture, no glowing edges, no light or shadow, no gradient or depth, no transparent areas, no checkerboard. Only the single J, no other lettering or objects, no presentation sheet.

### C / Serif

Use case: logo-brand. Design ONE minimal uppercase J monogram, candidate C for a contemporary experimental musician's website favicon.
A refined editorial serif J: tall straight right-hand stem, an elegant horizontal top serif, and a rounded leftward descender that gently curls upward into a short wedge-shaped terminal. Moderate thick/thin contrast, NOT hairline. Add just one discreet square step at the top serif's outer corner to suggest a digital cut. The overall silhouette must read a single J immediately. No extra floating dot, not lowercase j, not a fish.
One centered mark on a flat solid dark blue-black square canvas #0a151b, ivory mark #f7f3ea. Letter takes 70% of canvas height with generous consistent margin. Opaque background, no transparency.
Pristine flat vector-style typography. Absolutely no weathering, no glowing or bleeding edges, no texture, no border, no lighting, no shadows, no gradients or 3D, no scene, no presentation layout. Strong favicon clarity. Only text is J, no other text.
